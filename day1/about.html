About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture thoughts and ideas as they stike.
By giving you an eay to access digital tool to make notes on the go.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the "New note" field on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button.
  3. Find your new note in the "Your notes" list.
  4. Come back any time to review what you have written.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Note counter showing how many notes you have
